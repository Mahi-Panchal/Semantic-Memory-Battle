Rules
  1. On your turn, submit one word related to the current word.
  2. Cosine similarity must meet or exceed the difficulty threshold.
  3. Words already used in this game (or a simple variation) are banned.
  4. Time out or submit an unrelated word → lose a life.
  5. You win if all AI players are eliminated first.
  6. The game ends the moment you run out of lives.
